Lemon Music Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Lemon Music is an Android music player developed by the Lemon Music project. This Privacy Policy explains how Lemon Music handles information when you use the app.

We aim to collect and use only the information necessary to provide the features of Lemon Music.

2. Information We Access

Depending on the features you use, Lemon Music may access information required for music playback and YouTube integration.

Examples include:
  • Music stored on your device.
  • YouTube search and video information.
  • YouTube playlists and related account data when you connect your Google account.
  • Authentication information required to access authorized YouTube features.
  • Playback and application settings stored on your device.

3. Google and YouTube Account Access

Lemon Music can optionally connect to your Google account to provide YouTube-related features.

When you authorize this connection, Google provides Lemon Music with the permissions necessary for the features you have authorized.

Lemon Music uses this access only to provide the requested YouTube functionality, such as accessing playlists or other authorized YouTube information.

Lemon Music does not sell Google user data.

4. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by Lemon Music may be used to:

  • Play music.
  • Search for music.
  • Display YouTube content.
  • Access authorized playlists and account-related YouTube features.
  • Maintain playback and application settings.
  • Provide the features requested by the user.

5. Data Storage

Lemon Music may store application settings and authentication information locally on your device so that features can continue to work between app sessions.

You can remove locally stored application data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling Lemon Music.

6. Third-Party Services

Lemon Music may communicate with third-party services, including Google and YouTube services, when you use features that require them.

Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Lemon Music. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Lemon Music is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children for advertising or unrelated purposes.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Lemon Music's features or data practices change. The updated policy will be published on this page.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Lemon Music's privacy practices, please use the contact information provided for the Lemon Music project.
