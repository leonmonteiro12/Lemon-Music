🎵 About Lemon Music

Lemon Music is a lightweight Android music player designed to bring local music and YouTube music together in one place.

The goal is simple: make listening to music easy, fast and enjoyable while supporting features such as background playback, playlists, lyrics and media controls.

✨ Features

🎵 Music playback
▶️ Background playback
🔒 Screen-off playback
🔔 Media playback notification
⏮️ Previous / Next controls
⏯️ Play / Pause controls
⏩ Seeking
📱 Local music playback
🔎 YouTube music search
📋 Playlist support
🎤 Lyrics
🎧 Mini-player
🎶 Now Playing
🔀 Shuffle
🔁 Repeat
🔐 YouTube account integration

📱 Compatibility

Lemon Music is designed to support Android devices including older Android versions.

Minimum Android version: Android 6.0 (API 23)
